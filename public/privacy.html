Privacy Policy

This policy covers rclone-backup, a personal tool that backs up its owner's own working files to their own Google Drive. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

It asks only for the drive.file permission, which lets it see and change the files and folders it created itself. It cannot see any other file in the Drive.

What it does with data

It copies files between the owner's computer and the folder it created in the owner's Drive. Nothing is sent anywhere else, nothing is collected or stored by the tool, and nothing is shared with or sold to anyone.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
